## 4. Decisions needed

1. **Contact form email sending (Resend).**
   - **Sender address:** Resend needs a verified sender domain. Recommended: verify a sending subdomain such as `send.timdayconstruction.co.uk`. That adds DNS records at IONOS for the subdomain only, so the root MX, SPF and DMARC are untouched. Alternative: send from a domain you already have verified in Resend.
   - **Settings to add in Vercel:** `RESEND_API_KEY`, `CONTACT_FROM` (for example `Tim Day Construction <website@send.timdayconstruction.co.uk>`), and `CONTACT_TO` if it should go anywhere other than info@.
2. **Cloudflare Turnstile** (spam check on the form). It needs a free Cloudflare account, with the widget's hostnames set to timdayconstruction.co.uk and the preview domain.
   - **Settings to add in Vercel:** `PUBLIC_TURNSTILE_SITE_KEY` and `TURNSTILE_SECRET_KEY`.
   - **No-JavaScript visitors:** they won't pass the check, so the form also shows the phone number and email.
   - **Privacy policy:** add Turnstile when TDC next updates it.
3. **About page meta description:** the live one is 101 characters, below the 140–155 target. Keep it, or use this draft? "Meet Tim Day and the team behind Tim Day Construction: builders near Canterbury with over 20 years’ experience of new homes, extensions and veterinary fit-outs."
4. **Long titles kept verbatim (F11):** eight post titles exceed 60 characters even without the Yoast suffix (for example "What are the Benefits of Working with a Main Contractor from Start to Finish?"). Search engines will truncate them. Keep them, or shorten them now?
5. **Existing project meta descriptions** are the live Yoast text, kept verbatim. Several fall outside 140–155 characters (Vet Shack's is 208). Keep them, or have them redrafted?
