This residential project in Whitstable involved a thoughtfully designed side and rear extension to enhance living space and functionality. The ground floor features a polished concrete floor and a bespoke handcrafted kitchen, with exposed plywood finishes that highlight clean edges and craftsmanship whilst enhancing the existing traditional features. The kitchen is complemented by stainless steel worktops and sliding doors that open onto a newly landscaped garden, creating a seamless indoor-outdoor connection.

Upstairs, another bedroom was added, complete with vaulted ceiling, constructed using STEICO I-joists forming a timber I-beam portal frame without any tie members, giving a totally free interior volume. For thermal performance and moisture control, STEICO wood-fibre insulation was used throughout the roof and walls, providing both eco-efficiency and breathable protection.

This project showcases TDC’s high-quality craftsmanship, modern material use, and sustainable building methods, resulting in a warm, contemporary home that is both practical and beautifully finished.
