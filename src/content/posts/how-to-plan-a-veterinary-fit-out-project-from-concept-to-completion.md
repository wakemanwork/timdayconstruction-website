So, you want to start a veterinary fit out project, but not sure how? You have come to the right place!

At Tim Day Construction we specialise in delivering complete veterinary fit-out projects, managing every stage of the process to ensure your practice is functional, compliant, and beautifully finished, so take notes!

When it comes to creating a new veterinary practice or fitting out a current space, success lies in the detail. From the earliest design conversations to the moment, you open your doors to clients and their pets, attention to detail is key.

Why juggle five specialists, when one team can take care of it all?

We manage design, compliance, building control, fire strategy, and accessibility, all while ensuring your HVAC and M&E systems are expertly installed and your doors are ready to open on time.

Our approach brings every element together under one roof, so you can focus on prepping for your patients while we take care of the build.

## Design and Planning

Every successful veterinary fit-out begins with a solid plan. Come to us with your idea and we will collaborate closely with veterinary professionals to design layouts that enhance workflow, ensure patient comfort, and comply with RCVS standards. From consultation rooms and treatment areas to waiting spaces and staff facilities, every detail is tailored to your operational needs.

## Construction and Fit-Out

Once the design is approved, we manage the entire build process, from structural alterations and partitions to mechanical, electrical, and plumbing installations. Our experienced construction team coordinates all contractors and suppliers, ensuring the project stays on schedule and to budget without compromising on quality.

## Specialist Installations

Veterinary environments require a level of technical precision beyond typical commercial fit-outs. We handle all specialist installations including x-ray shielding, clinical-grade flooring, HVAC (Heating Ventilation Air Conditioning) and ventilation systems, and animal containment areas, ensuring your practice meets all health, safety, and hygiene regulations.

## Finishing Touches

The final stage is where your practice comes to life. We deliver high-quality finishes that create a welcoming, professional, and calming environment, designed with both clients and animals in mind. From lighting and decor to signage and furniture installation, we make sure every detail reflects your brand and purpose.

At Tim Day Construction, we believe with one partner comes one smooth process. From the first sketch to final handover, we make your veterinary vision a reality, built with care, precision, and an understanding of what your practice needs to thrive.
