## Frequently Asked Questions

### What does a Design & Build contractor do?

A Design & Build contractor provides a coordinated approach to both the design and construction stages of a project. At Tim Day Construction, we can become involved from the early stages, working alongside architects, engineers, designers and specialist consultants to help develop practical solutions, manage costs and ensure the design can be successfully delivered on site.

  

### When should I appoint a contractor for my new build?

Involving your contractor early can help identify potential construction challenges, programme considerations and cost implications before work begins on site. Early contractor involvement also allows greater coordination between the design team and those responsible for delivering the finished home.

  

### Do you build bespoke new homes in Kent?

Yes. Tim Day Construction delivers bespoke new build homes across Kent, working with clients and their professional teams from the early stages of the project through to construction and completion.

  

### Do you undertake major extensions and refurbishments?

Yes. Alongside new build homes, we deliver substantial residential extensions, refurbishments and remodelling projects. This can include significant alterations to an existing property, extensions, internal reconfiguration and the wider upgrading of the home.

  

### Can you manage my residential project from start to finish?

Yes. Depending on the requirements of your project, we can support the process from the early design and planning stages through construction, coordination of specialist trades and final completion. Our approach is centred around clear communication and careful project management throughout the build.

  

### Do you build energy-efficient homes?

Yes. We can work with your design team to deliver homes incorporating high-performance and energy-efficient construction methods. This can include careful consideration of insulation, airtightness, thermal performance and other measures designed to improve the overall energy performance and comfort of the finished home.

  

### Can you build a home using Passive House principles?

We can deliver residential projects incorporating Passive House principles where these form part of the project's design and specification. This may include enhanced insulation, improved airtightness, careful management of thermal bridging and other measures intended to reduce energy demand and improve building performance.

  

### Do you work with architects and structural engineers?

Yes. We regularly work alongside architects, structural engineers, designers and other specialist consultants. Close coordination between the professional team and contractor is particularly important on bespoke and technically detailed residential projects, helping ensure the design can be effectively translated into the finished build.
