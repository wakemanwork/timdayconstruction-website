### How long does a new build project typically take?

Timelines vary depending on the size and complexity of your project, but a typical single-dwelling new build in Kent can take between 6 and 12 months from breaking ground to completion. We provide offer a concept to completion service and handle everything from start to finish.

  

### Do you help with planning permission for new builds in Kent?

Absolutely. Our team can assist with the planning process, starting with us recommending local tried and tested Architects, picked to suit your particular project. There’s no point in choosing an architect who’s good at heritage projects but you want a modern beach side new build. Once on board we coordinate the whole process and liaise with Kent’s local authorities and architects to ensure your application meets current building regulations and planning requirements. We aim to make the entire process as smooth and stress-free as possible.
