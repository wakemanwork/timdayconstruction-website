## Change of use

[View Projects](https://timdayconstruction.co.uk/sector/change-of-use/)

A change of use project requires a deep understanding of both the property and the new purpose it will serve. Whether converting a commercial building into residential space or repurposing an industrial building, these projects come with unique challenges. Careful planning is essential to ensure compliance with laws, local authority restrictions and regulations, all while adapting the structure to its new purpose.

We handle every aspect, from securing approvals to managing timelines and budgets, ensuring a smooth transition. Our goal is to transform the property efficiently, meeting both regulatory standards and your vision, managing the process from start to finish.
