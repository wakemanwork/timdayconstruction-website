# Shipwright, Whitstable

When a local client sought to add more accommodation and modernise their beachfront property, while embracing the stunning views of the Thames Estuary, Tim Day Construction delivered a clever and stylish solution.

By creating an “upside-down” layout with bedrooms situated on the ground floor and living spaces on the first, the home now maximises the beautiful sunset views from all areas of the property. The living spaces on the top floor overlook and sea views, while the main bedroom below is fitted with en-suite facilities, and a decked terrace overlooking the seafront.

This coastal property is now let weekly as an elegant holiday home.

If your current residence no longer accommodates your needs, but you have an attachment to your current location or want to avoid the complexities of constructing a new home elsewhere, an extension could be the ideal solution for you.
