# Design & Build

At Tim Day Construction, our Design & Build service provides a complete, end-to-end solution for residential and commercial projects. By bringing both the design and construction elements together under one contract, we offer a streamlined process.

* Saving time
* Reducing costs
* Ensuring your vision is delivered with precision and care

## Design & Build Process

1. ### 1. Initial Enquiry

   We'll sit down with you to understand your goals, budget, timescales, and any site constraints.   
     
    Note: We carry professional indemnity insurance to protect you and yoru project. It providers cover in the unlikely event of any design errors or omissions, giving you added peace of mind throughout the process.
2. ### 2. Feasibility & site survey

   We carry out site inspections, utility checks, and feasibility studies to confirm your project is practical and achievable.
3. ### 3. Concept design & options

   We collaborate with a carefully selected group of trusted architects, choosing to one best suited to your specific project. Alongside our architects and designers, we develop idea, layouts and concepts to bring your vision to life. Together, we refine the design until it's perfect.
4. ### 4. Planning & Approvals

   We take care of planning applications, building control or listed building requirements, making sure everything is compliant.
5. ### 5. Detailed design & technical development

   Partnering with specialist engineers and suppliers, we create the technical drawings, specifications, and structural details needed to move forward.   
     
    Note: We carry professional indemnity insurance to protect you and yoru project. It providers cover in the unlikely event of any design errors or omissions, giving you added peace of mind throughout the process
6. ### 6. Finalising costs & value engineering

   We finalise costs with input from our trusted supply chain, finding smart ways to deliver the best value without compromising on quality.
7. ### 7. Contract sign-off & programme

   Once everything is agreed, we sign contracts, finalise the construction programme, and put all health & safety measures in place.
8. ### 8. Procurement & Ordering

   Using our established supplier relationships, we order key materials early to keep everything on track.
9. ### 9. Pre-construction set-up

   Site set up, mobilisation of plant and labour, pre-start meetings, and baseline surveys (asbestos, utilities etc). Client inductions and communications protocols are established.
10. ### 10. Construction & site management

    Our skilled team manages on-site works, coordinating directly with trusted subcontractors and suppliers to ensure consistent quality and progress.
11. ### 11. Testing and snagging

    We test and commission systems, carry out final inspections, and resolve any snags so everything runs smoothly.
12. ### 12. Handover

    We provide as-built drawings, manuals, warranties, and training before formally handing over your completed project.
13. ### 13. Aftercare & Maintenance

    We stay on hand to support you after completion, offering maintenance options through our trusted service partners.

Our Design & Build process is tailored to the unique needs of each project. While the core principles remain consistent, the specific stages, timelines, and level of detail can differ depending on the nature of the work, whether it’s a new build home or a specialist fit-out such as a veterinary practice. We’ll guide you through the exact steps relevant to your project from initial concept to final completion

* ### RIBA Stage 1:

  #### Strategic Definition

  Clarifying project objectives, feasibility and overall belief.
* ### RIBA Stage 2:

  #### Preparation & Briefing

  Developing the initial project brief, budget and programme, appointing the project team.
* ### RIBA Stage 3:

  #### Concept Design

  Creating initial design concepts, exploring layout options and agreeing the preferred design direction.
* ### RIBA Stage 4:

  #### Technical Design

  Producing detailed technical drawings and specifications ready for construction and tendering.
* ### RIBA Stage 5:

  #### Manufacturing & Construction

  Construction work on site, with ongoing design support and coordination.
* ### RIBA Stage 6:

  #### Handover

  Completing the building, client handover, and post-occupancy review.
* ### RIBA Stage 7:

  #### Use

  In-use assessment, maintenance, feedback, and ongoing building performance evaluation.
