# Whitstable Road, Canterbury

This luxury, detached house was designed specifically for its location, with classic, standard brickwork.

Inside, there are three reception rooms, one with a part-vaulted ceiling and two sets of French doors, overlooking and opening onto the gardens. The kitchen is fitted with a range of ‘Integra Soho’ units, completed by polished granite worksurfaces and a statement central island.

The staircase rises to the partially galleried first floor landing, which centres the five bedrooms, all of which are equipped with ensuites featuring automatic lighting and high-quality fittings and tiling.

Modern systems elevate the home’s comfort and efficiency, including a 16kW Samsung EHS Monobloc heat pump and energy-saving LED lighting. Security and convenience are prioritised with remote-controlled solid timber gates, a video intercom, and a permeable brick-paved driveway. New Builds are designed with the latest in energy-saving technologies and building materials, which leads to reduced utility bills and a smaller carbon footprint, while also complying with modern building standards.

The house has a NHBC Buildmark warranty with impressive specifications and meticulous detail. Every element of the project was thoughtfully planned, making it a standout property in every way.
