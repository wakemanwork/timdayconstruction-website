## Meet Our Founder

### I’m Tim Day, founder of Tim Day Construction.

I’ve been building houses for nearly 25 years, and for the last 20, I’ve been running TDC, learning, adapting, and fine-tuning what it means to build well. This hands-on experience, combined with a loyal team of skilled builders, allows us to deliver bespoke projects that go far beyond expectations.

From beautiful one-off homes to larger-scale commercial builds, we handle projects of all shapes and sizes. We have experience in Veterinary practices Based just outside Canterbury, we work across Kent, including Whitstable, Faversham, Herne Bay, and the surrounding areas.

We’re proud to be a company that’s big enough to manage £1m+ contracts, but small enough to care about the details. I personally take pride in making life easier for everyone on the project team, whether that’s an architect needing clear communication, or a PA chasing valuation paperwork that’s always quick, accurate, and hassle-free.

We’re not just here to get the job done, we’re here to make the whole experience enjoyable. No hidden costs, no stressful delays, just quality builds delivered by a team of friendly professionals. The kind of people you’ll want to celebrate with when the project is complete and recommend to anyone who’ll listen!

## Our Services

We deliver construction services with a focus on identifying project efficiencies, both in costs and timescale. By speaking to us from the concept stage, our collaboration with the designers will ensure plans and regulations are appropriately considered, before requesting local authority planning approvals and therefore reducing cost and potential for lost time.

We support from the earliest stages of planning and design and go on to manage the supply of materials, the right people resource and specialist services necessary for the project, through to overseeing the entire construction process to ensure project completion within budget and on schedule.

We work closely with architects, engineers, and provide skilled tradespeople to bring your vision to life. Every project we deliver will always be built safely, on time, and with the highest quality standards.
